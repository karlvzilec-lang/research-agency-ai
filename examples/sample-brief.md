# Client Brief: Prepaid Mobile Data Bundle Churn

**Client:** A mobile network operator in a competitive Southeast Asian market.

**Background:** Prepaid subscribers on mid-tier data bundles (2-5GB/week) are
churning to a competitor at a higher rate than any other segment, despite the
client's pricing being broadly comparable. Internal teams disagree on the cause:
Marketing believes it's a perception problem (competitor "feels" more generous),
Network Ops believes it's a real quality-of-service gap in certain provinces,
and Customer Care believes it's a top-up/renewal friction problem.

**Objective:** Identify the actual primary driver(s) of churn in this segment
and recommend the highest-impact interventions the client can realistically
ship within one quarter.

**Budget:** Mid-range engagement (mixed-methods preferred if it fits the
budget; the client wants defensible, decision-ready findings, not just a
perception study).

**Timeline:** Client wants topline findings within 6 weeks of kickoff.

**Stakeholders:** CMO (sponsor), Head of Network Ops, Head of Customer Care.
